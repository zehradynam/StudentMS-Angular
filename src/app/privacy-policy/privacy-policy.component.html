Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2024

Introduction

Student Management System ("StudentMS," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our student management platform.

By using StudentMS, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

Personal Information

We may collect personal information that can be used to identify you, including:

  • Name and Contact Information: Full name, email address, phone number
  • Account Information: Username, password, security questions
  • Professional Information: Role (student, teacher, administrator), department, class assignments
  • Academic Information: Grades, attendance records, course enrollment, performance metrics
Usage Data

We automatically collect information about how you use our platform:

  • Log data (access times, pages viewed, IP address)
  • Device information (browser type, operating system)
  • User interactions and feature usage
  • Performance and error data
Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic site functionality
  • Performance Cookies: Help us understand how our site is used
  • Functional Cookies: Remember your preferences and settings

How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

Core Services
  • Provide and maintain the StudentMS platform
  • Process student and teacher records
  • Manage academic data and reports
  • Facilitate communication between users
Support and Improvement
  • Respond to user inquiries and support requests
  • Improve our services and user experience
  • Monitor platform performance and security
  • Develop new features and functionality
Legal and Safety
  • Comply with legal obligations and regulations
  • Protect against fraud and unauthorized access
  • Ensure platform security and integrity
  • Enforce our terms of service

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We may share your information under the following circumstances:

Within the Educational Institution
  • Authorized administrators, teachers, and staff
  • Academic advisors and counselors
  • Other students when appropriate (e.g., class directories)
Service Providers
  • Cloud hosting and infrastructure providers
  • Payment processing services (if applicable)
  • Analytics and support tools
Legal Requirements
  • When required by law or legal process
  • To protect our rights, property, or safety
  • In emergency situations
Important: We never sell your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your information:

Technical Security
  • SSL/TLS encryption for data transmission
  • Encrypted data storage
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Firewall and intrusion detection
Administrative Security
  • Role-based access controls
  • Employee background checks
  • Regular security training
  • Incident response procedures

While we take reasonable precautions, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to:

  • Fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Resolve disputes and enforce agreements
  • Maintain accurate academic and administrative records

When data is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymize it in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

Access and Correction
  • View your personal information
  • Request corrections to inaccurate data
  • Update your profile information
Control and Deletion
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt-out of non-essential data collection
  • Restrict processing of your information

To exercise these rights, please contact our privacy team using the information below.

Children's Privacy

StudentMS is designed for educational institutions and may collect information from minors. We comply with applicable laws regarding children's privacy, including:

  • COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) for users under 13
  • FERPA (Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act) for educational records
  • State and local regulations regarding student data

Parental consent is required for collecting personal information from minors under applicable laws.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes:

  • We will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
  • We will notify users of significant changes via email or platform notification
  • We may require your consent for material changes

Your continued use of StudentMS after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
